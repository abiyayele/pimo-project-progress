Current knowledge migration design

Safe transition from live company Drive to Pimo knowledge.

1 · Keep Google Drive as the working source

The team continues working normally in Google Drive while the NAS receives a read-only-source mirror. Initial transfers use rclone copy semantics so deletions in Drive do not automatically delete NAS copies.

2 · Mirror incrementally to NAS

New and modified files will be pulled on a schedule to Volume 1. The initial dataset is approximately 212+ GiB across 484.7k objects.

3 · Index selectively

Folders 01, 02, 03, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 are planned for default indexing. Management and personnel data remain restricted; software/backups and non-knowledge binaries remain mirror-only.

4 · Re-index only what changes

PostgreSQL will track document identity, path, modification state and indexing status. New or changed documents will be reprocessed incrementally instead of rebuilding the full knowledge base.

Next delivery sequence
