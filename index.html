Knowledge migration design

Current agreed operating model.

1 · Google Drive remains the team workspace

Staff continue adding and editing files in Google Drive. Pimo reads from a controlled NAS mirror rather than forcing a workflow change.

2 · NAS mirrors changes incrementally

Initial transfer uses rclone copy semantics. New and modified files will later be pulled on a recurring schedule without automatically deleting NAS copies.

3 · Classify before embedding

Useful business documents are parsed and indexed; software files, binaries, archives, media assets and low-value technical artifacts remain stored but are not embedded.

4 · Re-index only what changes

PostgreSQL will track file identity and indexing state so new or modified documents are processed incrementally instead of rebuilding the entire corpus.
